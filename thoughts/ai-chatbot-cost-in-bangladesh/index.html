How Much Does an AI Chatbot Cost in Bangladesh? (2026 Guide)

By Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah) · 28 November 2026 · 3 min read

  • AI chatbot price Bangladesh
  • AI agent cost
  • chatbot development Bangladesh
  • small business
  • Dhaka

Short answer

  • A useful AI chatbot has two costs: a one-time build and a monthly running cost (AI usage, hosting, and someone checking it).
  • Bangladeshi AI agencies listed on Clutch and DesignRush in late 2026 start their projects at about $1,000 (roughly ৳1.2 lakh); freelancers on Fiverr and Upwork often quote less for a single workflow.
  • The biggest price drivers are how many channels the bot answers on, how many systems it connects to, and how much Bangla and Banglish it must understand.
  • The cheapest good start is one channel, your 20 most common questions, and a human who reviews anything unusual.

"Bhai, ekta chatbot banate koto lagbe?" — it is the first thing most business owners want to know. The honest answer is: it depends on what you want the bot to do. But "it depends" is not useful, so here is how the cost actually breaks down in Bangladesh in 2026 — and how to keep your first one small and cheap.

What are you actually paying for?

Every AI chatbot or AI agent has two kinds of cost:

  1. The build (one time). Designing the conversations, writing the answers, connecting the bot to your Facebook Page, website or WhatsApp, connecting it to your product list or sheet, and testing it with real customer messages.
  2. Running it (every month). The AI model is paid per use, the bot needs somewhere to run, and someone has to read the conversations it got wrong and improve them.

Most people only ask about the first one. The second one decides whether the bot is still useful three months later.

What do agencies and freelancers in Bangladesh charge?

Public price information is thin, but directory listings give a useful floor. In November 2026:

  • Karigor AI Labs (Dhaka) lists a minimum project size of $1,000 and an hourly rate of $50–$99 on Clutch.
  • ByteFlow AI Labs (Dhaka) lists a minimum budget in the $1,000–$10,000 range on DesignRush.
  • Dozens of Bangladeshi freelancers on Fiverr and Upwork sell single n8n workflows and chatbot builds, usually for less than an agency minimum, with prices that change often.

So a reasonable expectation: an agency project starts around ৳1.2 lakh and goes up with scope; a freelancer can be cheaper for one narrow job. These are listed prices, not quotes — always ask for a written scope.

What makes a chatbot more expensive?

Five things move the price more than anything else:

  1. Channels. One Facebook Page is simpler than Page + Instagram + WhatsApp + website.
  2. Connections. Answering from a fixed list of questions is simple. Checking live stock, prices or order status in your system takes more work.
  3. Language. Bangla, Banglish and English in the same inbox need more testing. "dam koto", "price koto", "koto taka" and "দাম কত" are the same question — the bot has to know that. (I wrote about this in Why I Build AI That Speaks Bangla, Banglish and English.)
  4. Actions. Replying is cheaper than taking orders, booking visits or updating records.
  5. Risk. Anything about money, legal matters or property documents needs a human check built in, which adds design time.

How do you keep the first chatbot cheap?

Start smaller than you think:

  • One channel — usually your Facebook Page, because that is where most Bangladeshi customers message first.
  • Your 20 most common questions, copied exactly as customers type them, Banglish and spelling mistakes included.
  • A clear hand-off: anything outside those questions goes to a person, with the conversation already summarised.
  • Two weeks of watching before it answers on its own.

That version is small enough to build quickly and cheap to run, and it shows you whether a bigger bot is worth paying for. It is also how I structure every project — I explain the steps in What Does an AI Workflow Builder Actually Do?

What should you ask before paying anyone?

  • Will it understand Banglish and mixed Bangla-English messages? Ask to see it handle five of your real messages.
  • What is the monthly cost after launch, and who pays for AI usage?
  • Who owns the bot, the prompts and the data if you stop working together?
  • What happens when the bot does not know an answer?
  • Can you see every conversation it had?

If the answers are vague, the price is not the problem.

Quick answers

How much does an AI chatbot cost in Bangladesh?

It depends on scope. Bangladeshi AI agencies listed on Clutch and DesignRush in November 2026 state minimum projects of about $1,000 (roughly ৳1.2 lakh), while freelancers on Fiverr and Upwork often quote less for a single workflow. Expect a monthly running cost on top for AI usage and hosting.

Is there a monthly cost for an AI chatbot?

Yes. The AI model is paid per use, the bot needs hosting, and someone should review its answers. For a small business answering a few hundred messages a day, AI usage is usually the smallest part; the time spent improving answers matters more.

Can I get a cheap chatbot that understands Banglish?

Yes, if you keep the first version small: one channel such as your Facebook Page, your most common questions written the way customers actually type them, and clear hand-off to a person. Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah), an AI workflow builder in Dhaka, designs agents this way.
