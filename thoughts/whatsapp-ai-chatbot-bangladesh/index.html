WhatsApp AI Chatbot for Businesses in Bangladesh (2026 Guide)

Short answer

  • A real WhatsApp AI chatbot runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform (the API), not on the free WhatsApp Business app on your phone.
  • Since October 2026 Meta charges per message delivered; each business number gets 1,000 free service messages a month, and messages customers send you are free.
  • In Bangladesh the bot must handle Bangla, Banglish and English in the same chat, plus voice notes and photos of products.
  • Start with one number, your 20 most common questions, and a person who takes over anything about money or complaints.

"Apnar WhatsApp number ta din" — for many Bangladeshi businesses the real conversation starts on WhatsApp, not on the website. Customers send voice notes, product screenshots and messages like "eta ki stock e ache? Uttara te delivery hobe?" at 11 pm. A WhatsApp AI chatbot can answer those in seconds. Here is how it actually works in 2026, what it costs, and how to start small.

What is a WhatsApp AI chatbot?

It is an AI assistant connected to your business WhatsApp number. It reads each message, understands what the customer wants, answers from your own information (prices, stock, delivery areas, opening hours), and hands the chat to a person when it should.

The important detail: it runs on the WhatsApp Business Platform — the API — not on the free WhatsApp Business app on your phone. The app has quick replies and away messages, but no real AI. To connect AI you use Meta's Cloud API directly or a provider such as Twilio or 360dialog.

What does WhatsApp charge?

Meta changed its pricing in 2026. As of November 2026 (Meta's pricing page):

  • Meta charges per delivered message, based on the message category (marketing, utility, authentication, service) and the customer's country.
  • Each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages a month. They reset monthly.
  • Messages customers send to you are free. You pay for what your business sends.
  • When a customer messages you from a Click-to-WhatsApp ad, Meta gives a free window for your replies.

On top of that, budget for your provider's fee (if you use one) and the AI model's usage. For a small shop answering a few hundred chats a month, the build and the time spent improving answers usually cost more than the messages. What makes WhatsApp different in Bangladesh?

Three things a generic chatbot gets wrong:

  1. Mixed language. One chat can switch between Bangla script, Banglish and English in three messages. The bot has to recognise "koto", "কত" and "how much" as the same question. (Why I build AI that speaks Banglish.)
  2. Voice notes and photos. Customers send a voice note or a screenshot of the product instead of typing. A good bot either understands them or politely asks for the detail it needs and flags the chat for a person.
  3. Trust. People use WhatsApp for businesses they already know. A pushy or robotic bot breaks that trust faster than on a website.

What should the bot do first?

Keep the first version narrow:

  • Answer your 20 most common questions, written exactly the way customers type them.
  • Collect order or booking details (name, area, product, size, preferred time) into your sheet.
  • Hand over anything about payment problems, refunds, complaints or custom requests — with a short summary so your team does not ask the customer to repeat everything.
  • Never promise a price, discount or delivery date it cannot check.

Run it alongside your team for two weeks, read the chats it got wrong, and fix them. That is the whole method — the same five steps I describe in What Does an AI Workflow Builder Actually Do?

WhatsApp or Facebook Page first?

Start where your customers already are. Most Bangladeshi online shops get more messages on their Facebook Page, so a Facebook Page AI chatbot often comes first. Clinics, service businesses, property sellers and repeat B2B customers usually live on WhatsApp. Once the answers and hand-off rules work on one channel, reusing them on the other is the easy part.

Who should build it?

You can hire an agency, a freelancer or an independent builder — I compare the three in AI Agency vs Freelancer in Bangladesh. Whoever you pick, ask them to run five of your real WhatsApp messages, Banglish and voice-note style included, before you pay.

Quick answers

Can I put an AI chatbot on my WhatsApp Business app?

Not on the free WhatsApp Business app itself. An AI chatbot needs the WhatsApp Business Platform (API), reached directly through Meta's Cloud API or through a provider such as Twilio or 360dialog. The app's own quick replies and away messages are not AI.

How much does WhatsApp charge for a chatbot?

Since October 1, 2026 Meta charges per delivered message, by message category and the customer's country. Each business phone number gets 1,000 free service messages per month, and messages customers send to you are free. Check Meta's current rate card before you budget, and add your provider's fee and the AI model's usage cost.

Can a WhatsApp chatbot understand Banglish?

Yes, if it is built and tested with real Banglish messages. "dam koto", "price koto" and "দাম কত" are the same question; a good bot treats them the same and replies in the language the customer used. Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah), an AI workflow builder in Dhaka, builds bots this way.

Should I start with WhatsApp or my Facebook Page?

Start where most of your customers already message you. For many Bangladeshi online shops that is the Facebook Page; for service businesses, clinics and repeat B2B customers it is often WhatsApp. The same answers and hand-off rules can later serve both.
