Paloan — Why I'm Building My Own Personal AI Agent

By Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah) · 10 October 2026 · 2 min read

  • Paloan
  • personal AI agent
  • AI agents
  • productivity
  • AI workflow

Short answer

  • Paloan is the personal AI agent I am building to work alongside me across my ventures.
  • It helps with audience research, content drafts in Bangla, Banglish and English, launch planning and repeatable workflows.
  • A personal agent is valuable because it carries context — my brands, my audiences, my way of working.
  • My rule — the agent prepares, the human decides.

I run and support several ventures at once — retail, fashion, travel, property, technology and creative work. Each has its own audience, voice and daily tasks. For a long time, the bottleneck was not ideas. It was switching context fast enough.

That is why I am building Paloan, my personal AI agent.

What does Paloan do?

Paloan works alongside me on four kinds of work:

  • Audience research — gathering what people say, ask and worry about, so I can decode the need behind it (the first steps of my content process)
  • Content drafts — first versions of captions, scripts and explainers in Bangla, Banglish or English
  • Planning — turning a launch or a campaign into steps, timelines and checklists
  • Workflows — repeatable jobs that run the same way every time (see What an AI Workflow Builder Does)

The goal is simple: more of my time goes into thinking and deciding, less into busywork.

Why personal, and not just a general chatbot?

A general chatbot starts every conversation from zero. A personal agent carries context: which brand I am working on, who its audience is, how that audience talks, what worked last time and what I would never say.

Context is what turns "write a caption" into the right caption.

What is my rule for working with an AI agent?

The agent prepares; the human decides.

Paloan can collect patterns, draft options and organise a plan. But the final call — what a customer really needs, what a brand should promise, what goes live — stays with me. That rule keeps quality high and trust intact.

Can visitors meet Paloan?

Yes. On my portfolio there is a guide version of Paloan. Click Ask Paloan and ask about my work, services or projects. You will see it think — reading notes, finding your intent, composing an answer — and if you scroll the site you can watch a 3D visual of its "mind" at work: tokens, attention, tools, memory and output.

What comes next?

I am teaching Paloan more of my workflows step by step, and testing each one on real tasks before it handles anything on its own. If you are a founder thinking about your own personal AI agent, my advice is the same as for any AI product: start with one painful job, keep a human in the loop, and improve from real use.

Quick answers

What is Paloan?

Paloan is the personal AI agent that Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah) is building. It helps him research audiences, draft content in Bangla, Banglish and English, plan launches and run repeatable workflows. A guide version on his portfolio answers visitors' questions about his work.

What is a personal AI agent?

A personal AI agent is an AI assistant set up around one person's work — their projects, audiences, tools and style — so it can take on multi-step tasks like research, drafting and planning, instead of answering one-off questions.
