I run and support several ventures at once — retail, fashion, travel, property, technology and creative work. Each has its own audience, voice and daily tasks. For a long time, the bottleneck was not ideas. It was **switching context** fast enough.

That is why I am building **Paloan**, my personal AI agent.

## What does Paloan do?

Paloan works alongside me on four kinds of work:

- **Audience research** — gathering what people say, ask and worry about, so I can decode the need behind it (the first steps of [my content process](../content-starts-in-the-audiences-head/))
- **Content drafts** — first versions of captions, scripts and explainers in Bangla, Banglish or English
- **Planning** — turning a launch or a campaign into steps, timelines and checklists
- **Workflows** — repeatable jobs that run the same way every time (see [What an AI Workflow Builder Does](../what-an-ai-workflow-builder-does/))

The goal is simple: more of my time goes into thinking and deciding, less into busywork.

## Why personal, and not just a general chatbot?

A general chatbot starts every conversation from zero. A personal agent carries **context**: which brand I am working on, who its audience is, how that audience talks, what worked last time and what I would never say.

Context is what turns "write a caption" into the *right* caption.

## What is my rule for working with an AI agent?

**The agent prepares; the human decides.**

Paloan can collect patterns, draft options and organise a plan. But the final call — what a customer really needs, what a brand should promise, what goes live — stays with me. That rule keeps quality high and trust intact.

## Can visitors meet Paloan?

Yes. On [my portfolio](../../#paloan) there is a guide version of Paloan. Click **Ask Paloan** and ask about my work, services or projects. You will see it think — reading notes, finding your intent, composing an answer — and if you scroll the site you can watch a 3D visual of its "mind" at work: tokens, attention, tools, memory and output.

## What comes next?

I am teaching Paloan more of my workflows step by step, and testing each one on real tasks before it handles anything on its own. If you are a founder thinking about your own personal AI agent, my advice is the same as for any [AI product](../building-mimai-with-claude-code/): start with one painful job, keep a human in the loop, and improve from real use.
