In Bangladesh, a huge share of online selling happens in one place: the **Facebook Page inbox**. Customers see a post, tap "Send message", and type something like *"Apu eta koto?"* or *"Size L ache? Dhaka te delivery kobe?"*. If nobody answers in a few minutes, many of them move on to the next Page.

That makes the Page inbox the best first place for an AI chatbot.

## Why don't normal auto-replies work?

Facebook already lets you set automated responses and instant replies. They are useful for "Thanks, we'll reply soon" — but they match fixed keywords or send the same message to everyone.

Bangladeshi customers do not write fixed keywords. The same question arrives as:

- *dam koto* · *price koto* · *koto taka* · *দাম কত* · *price?*

A keyword rule catches one or two of these. An **AI chatbot** reads the meaning, so all five get the right answer — in the language the customer used. (Why this matters so much: [Why I Build AI That Speaks Bangla, Banglish and English](../ai-that-speaks-banglish/).)

## What can a Facebook Page AI chatbot do?

A well-built Page bot handles the repetitive 80% of the inbox:

1. **Answer product questions** — price, size, colour, fabric, availability — from your own product list, not from guesswork.
2. **Explain delivery and payment** — inside Dhaka vs outside, delivery charge, cash on delivery, bKash.
3. **Collect order details** — name, phone, address, product, size — and send them to your order sheet or team.
4. **Hand off cleanly** — anything unusual (a complaint, a custom order, a bargaining customer) goes to a person, with a one-line summary so they do not have to scroll.
5. **Reply after hours** — so the 11 pm message is answered at 11 pm, not at 10 am.

What it should **not** do on its own: promise discounts, accept returns, or say anything about money that a person has not approved.

## How does it work behind the scenes?

You do not need to understand the technology, but it helps to know the parts:

- **Connection** — the bot is connected to your Page through Meta's Messenger platform, with your permission as Page admin.
- **Knowledge** — your products, prices, delivery rules and FAQ, kept in a simple sheet you can edit.
- **The AI model** — reads each message, finds the right answer in your knowledge, and writes a reply in Bangla, Banglish or English.
- **Rules** — what it may answer alone and what must go to a person.
- **Review** — every conversation is saved so you can check what it said and improve it.

## How do you set one up without wasting money?

1. **Export or copy your last 100 inbox conversations.** Pick the 20 questions that come up most, written exactly as customers typed them.
2. **Write the perfect answer for each**, in the tone your best salesperson uses.
3. **Decide the hand-off rules**: what the bot must never answer alone.
4. **Run it in "draft mode" first** — the bot suggests replies, a person sends them — for one or two weeks.
5. **Then let it answer** the common questions on its own, and keep reviewing.

## Is it worth it for a small Page?

If your inbox gets the same questions dozens of times a day, yes — the time saved and the late-night replies pay for a small bot quickly. If you get five messages a day, keep answering them yourself and use AI to draft replies instead.
