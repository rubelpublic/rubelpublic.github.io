n8n vs Make vs Zapier: Which Should a Bangladeshi Business Use?

By Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah) · 28 November 2026 · 2 min read

  • n8n vs Make vs Zapier
  • n8n Bangladesh
  • workflow automation tools
  • AI automation Bangladesh
  • no-code automation
  • small business

Short answer

  • All three connect your apps and run steps automatically; the big difference is how they count usage and who runs the server.
  • Zapier counts every action (task), Make counts operations (credits), and n8n counts a whole workflow run as one execution — so long workflows get cheaper on n8n.
  • n8n can also be self-hosted for free on your own server, which suits Bangladeshi businesses paying in dollars, but someone has to maintain it.
  • For a first simple automation, Make or Zapier is quicker; for AI agents, Banglish chatbots and high volume, n8n usually wins.

"Bhai, n8n shikhbo na Zapier?" — I get this question from business owners and from students in my AI course. The honest answer depends less on features and more on two things: how each tool counts your usage, and who keeps it running. Here is the plain-language version for a business in Bangladesh, as of November 2026.

What do these tools actually do?

All three are workflow automation tools. You connect apps — Facebook Page, Google Sheets, Gmail, WhatsApp, your website — and tell the tool: when this happens, do these steps. For example: when a new order message arrives, read it with AI, add the details to a sheet, and notify the team on WhatsApp.

The steps are similar on all three. The bill is not.

How does each one charge?

What it counts Each action ("task") Each operation ("credit") One whole workflow run ("execution")

Prices change often — check Zapier, Make and n8n before you decide. What matters is the counting:

A workflow with 10 steps that runs 1,000 times a month is roughly 9,000–10,000 units on Zapier or Make, but 1,000 executions on n8n. Long workflows — which AI workflows usually are — get expensive fast when every step is counted.

Why does this matter more in Bangladesh?

  • Dollar billing. All three bill in USD (n8n Cloud in EUR in some regions). A plan that looks small becomes real money in taka, and international card limits can be a problem.
  • Self-hosting. n8n can run on your own server for the cost of hosting. For a business paying in taka, that can be the difference between "we can afford automation" and "we can't". The trade-off: someone must update and back it up.
  • Language. Banglish chatbots need more steps — understand the message, match it to your answers, decide, reply, log. That plays to n8n's per-execution model and its AI-agent features.

Which should you pick?

  • Zapier if you want one or two simple automations working today, and nobody on the team is technical.
  • Make if you like a visual canvas, need branches and loops, and want a more generous free plan.
  • n8n if you are building AI agents or chatbots, run many steps or high volume, care about where your data lives, or want to self-host to keep costs in check.

Many businesses end up with Make or Zapier for small office tasks and n8n for the serious, customer-facing workflows.

The tool is the easy part

The hard part is not choosing the tool; it is designing the workflow — what triggers it, what the AI should understand, what it may decide alone and when a person takes over. Quick answers

Which is cheapest for a Bangladeshi small business, n8n, Make or Zapier?

For a few simple automations, the free plans of Make (1,000 credits a month) or Zapier (100 tasks a month) may cost nothing. For long, multi-step or high-volume workflows, n8n is usually cheapest because one full workflow run counts as one execution, and the self-hosted Community edition is free apart from server costs.

Is n8n really free?

The self-hosted n8n Community edition is free to use for your own business under n8n's Sustainable Use License; you pay for the server and someone's time to maintain it. n8n Cloud, where n8n hosts it for you, is a paid monthly plan.

Which tool is best for an AI chatbot that understands Banglish?

Any of the three can call an AI model, but n8n gives the most control over AI agents, memory and custom logic, which helps when a bot must handle Bangla, Banglish and English in one inbox. Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah), an AI workflow builder in Dhaka, uses this kind of setup for chatbots and workflows.

Do I need a developer to use n8n?

Not for simple workflows, but n8n has a steeper learning curve than Zapier or Make, and self-hosting needs someone comfortable with servers. Many businesses have a builder set it up and then manage day-to-day changes themselves.
