AI Power User: a Practical AI Course in Bangla

Learn to use AI for real work — choosing the right tool, prompting in Bangla, Banglish and English, creating content and automating the tasks you repeat every week. Every module ends with something built, tested or automated.

Short answer

  • 10 classes, 18–20 hours, taught in Bangla by Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah) in Dhaka.
  • About 70% hands-on practice, from beginner to intermediate.
  • Six modules: Understand, Use, Create, Automate and Think with AI, plus a capstone.
  • For individual learners and for company teams.

Who is it for?

Business owners, marketers, freelancers, students and teams in Bangladesh who already use ChatGPT a little and want to use AI properly in their daily work. No coding needed.

What you will learn

  1. Understand AI. What today's AI models can and cannot do, how they work in plain language, and how to choose the right model or tool for a job instead of using one chatbot for everything.
  2. Use AI. Prompting that gets useful answers in Bangla, Banglish and English: giving context, examples and constraints, checking the output, and fixing it when it is wrong.
  3. Create with AI. Writing, images, audio and video with AI: drafting posts, scripts and visuals for real projects, and keeping your own voice and brand in the result.
  4. Automate with AI. Turning a task you repeat every week into a workflow with tools such as n8n, Make or Zapier: trigger, understand, decide, act, check.
  5. Think with AI. Using AI for research, planning and decisions: breaking down problems, comparing options and spotting when the AI is confidently wrong.
  6. Capstone: your Personal AI Operating System. You design and build your own set of AI tools, prompts and workflows for your work or business, and present it working.

How it is taught

The focus is on choosing the right model or tool, designing repeatable workflows and producing useful work — not memorizing buttons that change every month. Examples come from real Bangladeshi businesses: Facebook Page inboxes full of Banglish, product content, property enquiries and online shops. I use the same methods in my own ventures and client work; see AI automation and n8n vs Make vs Zapier.
