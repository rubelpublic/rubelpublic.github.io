FAQ · Quick answers

Questions people ask about Rapar Rubel.

Short, direct answers about who I am, what I do and how to work with me.

Who is Rapar Rubel?

Rapar Rubel is the online name of Rubel Miah, a business builder, AI and product operator based in Dhaka, Bangladesh. He works across business strategy, AI workflows, digital products, automation, branding, content and venture operations, and tests his work inside real ventures.

Are Rapar Rubel, Rubel Miah and Rubel Public the same person?

Yes. Rubel Miah is his real name, Rapar Rubel is his online and creative name, and Rubel Public is another public identity he uses, including for this portfolio at rubelpublic.github.io and on GitHub.

What services does Rapar Rubel offer?

Business strategy (business models, pricing, offers and operating plans), practical AI agents and workflow automation, websites and web apps, digital marketing and growth, brand and creative systems, AI video direction, and AI training for teams in Bangla and English.

What has Rapar Rubel built?

His work includes MimAI (an AI file assistant), Proyojon Abashon (land share and property management in Dhaka), Proyojon Prozukti (technology and growth services), Proyojon Solutions (legal-tech advisory), Proyojon Shop (retail and import), The AB Wardrobe (women's fashion), Proyojon Vromon (travel) and Gaan Banai (a creative-AI music concept).

Can he build AI tools that understand Bangla and Banglish?

Yes. Building AI that works in Bangla, Banglish and English is a core focus. MimAI, for example, finds files in a user's own Google Drive from questions written in any of the three.

What is the AI Power User course?

AI Power User is a practical AI course taught in Bangla for Bangladeshi learners and teams: 10 classes, 18 to 20 hours, about 70% hands-on practice, from beginner to intermediate. Every module ends with something built, tested or automated.
