How I think · Content & audience psychology

Content starts in the audience’s head, not on the brand’s page.

Before I write a line or design a frame, I work out how the audience thinks: the words they use, what they worry about, what makes them stop scrolling and what makes them trust. Then I build the content to fit that mind—in Bangla, Banglish or English.

Audience mind map · how I’d read it

They say

They mean

They fear

They share

So the content becomes

  1. 01

    Listen

    Read comments, inbox questions, reviews and search terms. Collect the exact words people use, not the words a brand prefers.

  2. 02

    Decode

    Find the need under the request: the fear, the hope, the status, the price anxiety. Separate what people say from what they mean.

  3. 03

    Frame

    Pick one angle and one emotion. Position the brand as the answer to a need, not a product looking for buyers.

  4. 04

    Create

    Write and design native to the platform and the language: Banglish captions, Bangla explainers, carousels, short AI video, product-led creative.

  5. 05

    Test & systemise

    Ship variations, watch saves, shares, replies and sales, then turn what worked into a repeatable content system.
