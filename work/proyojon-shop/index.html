Proyojon Shop: Building a China-to-Bangladesh Retail Business and Its Online Store

Case study by Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah)

Proyojon Shop sells toys, stationery, bags and accessories sourced in China across Bangladesh. It is one of my own ventures, and the place where I learned to make the commercial logic add up before scaling anything.

At a glance

  • Business: Retail and import — toys, stationery, bags and accessories
  • Market: Bangladesh, sourced from China
  • My role: Business model, pricing logic, category strategy, product content and creative
  • Website: proyojon.shop

What was the job?

An import business lives or dies on its numbers. A product that looks cheap at the factory can lose money once shipping, duties, packaging and delivery are added. The job was to build a retail operation where every product's real cost is known — and an online store where customers can find and trust those products.

What did I do?

  • Costing from factory to shelf. Every product is costed and tracked from the factory price to the shelf, so pricing decisions are based on the full cost, not the purchase price.
  • Category strategy. Toys, stationery, bags and accessories organised the way Bangladeshi shoppers look for them.
  • Product content and creative. Product content, creative QA and campaign assets for the store and social media.
  • The online store. proyojon.shop brings the categories, product content and pricing logic together in one place.

What did it teach me?

Proyojon Shop (with its retail arm, Proyojon 360) is the proof behind my strategy work: business models, pricing, offers and operating plans that add up before execution scales.
