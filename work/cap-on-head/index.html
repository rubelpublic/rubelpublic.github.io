CAP ON HEAD: Content and Product Photography That Lets Every Cap Sell Itself

Case study by Rapar Rubel (Rubel Miah)

CAP ON HEAD is a Bangladesh cap brand. I run its content, product photography and product presentation — how every cap looks, reads and sells on social media and on caponhead.com.

At a glance

  • Brand: CAP ON HEAD, a cap brand in Bangladesh
  • Category: Fashion accessories — caps
  • My role: Content, product photography and product presentation
  • Channels: Social media and caponhead.com

What was the job?

A cap is a product people buy with their eyes. Online, the photo and a few lines of text have to do everything a shop assistant would: show the shape, the colour, the fit and the feeling. The job was to make every cap present itself clearly — on a phone screen, in a feed, and on the product page.

What do I do?

  • Product-led photography. Every cap's photo and angle is planned for how it will look on social media and on the website, so the product sells itself.
  • Product descriptions. Each cap's description is planned alongside its photo, so the two tell the same story.
  • Website merchandising. How caps are presented and grouped on caponhead.com.
  • Social content. Product-led posts for the brand's social media channels.

How does this fit my content process?

It is step 4 — Create — of the five-step process I use for every brand: listen, decode, frame, create, test. For a visual product the "create" step leans on photography rather than long copy. I explain the whole process in Content Starts in the Audience's Head.
